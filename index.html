TCS Expectations

Challenge Catalogue • Core Principles

This is an AI engineering hackathon, not a chatbot-building competition. A chatbot, static dashboard, prompt wrapper, API integration or conventional ML prediction by itself is not considered a complete solution.

Core Capability Criteria

Teams are expected to demonstrate at least 5 of these 7 capabilities:

  • Reason across multiple sources.
  • Plan multiple steps toward an objective.
  • Act through tools, APIs, functions or simulated enterprise systems.
  • Verify conclusions against evidence.
  • Adapt when circumstances or evidence change.
  • Human Control for consequential decisions.
  • Evaluate performance using measurable metrics.
Dynamic Jury Scenario: Every challenge also includes an unseen jury scenario during final evaluation.

A solution consisting primarily of any of the following will not automatically qualify as a complete AI solution:

  • Chatbot UI
  • Prompt + LLM response
  • Static dashboard
  • Power BI/dashboard visualization only
  • Basic RAG Q&A
  • Single API call
  • Prepared demonstration with hardcoded results

Submission Requirements

Standard 8-Pillar Evaluation Criteria

1. Problem Understanding Presentation

Explain the real problem and stakeholders. Have less no of slide (max 5 slides) to articluate your AI work but mandatory to coverer Architecture ,Working Prototype ,Dynamic Adaptation

2. AI Architecture

Show: Input → AI/Agents → Tools → Data/Knowledge → Decisions → Human Control → Output

3. Working Prototype

A functional implementation is required.

4. AI Reasoning

Demonstrate why AI made its recommendation.

5. Evidence & Traceability

Show which evidence influenced important conclusions.

6. Evaluation

Define measurable metrics. Examples:

  • accuracy
  • groundedness
  • latency
  • cost
  • false-positive rate
  • task-success rate
  • recovery time
  • business KPI.
7. Responsible AI

Explain:

  • privacy
  • security
  • hallucination control
  • human approval
  • failure handling
  • bias/fairness where applicable.
8. Dynamic Adaptation

Demonstrate how the solution handles the jury's surprise scenario.

Evaluation Criteria

Mentor Evaluation & Jury Evaluation

Stage 1: Mentor Technical Evaluation

Teams will first undergo a technical evaluation by mentors. The maximum score is 50 marks.

Category Marks
Problem and Challenge Understanding 10
AI Reasoning and Solution Architecture 10
Technical Implementation and Prototype 10
Evidence, Evaluation and Responsible AI 10
Adaptability, Innovation and Impact 10
Total 50

Stage 2: Jury Presentation Scoring

10 shortlisted teams will present to 5 jury members. Each jury member will independently evaluate each team for a maximum of 100 marks.

Category Maximum
Solution Articulation and Presentation 20
Problem Statement Understanding 15
Solution Hypothesis 20
Technical Excellence and Working Prototype 25
Business Impact Assessment 20
Total per Jury Member 100
